Collaborations go best when both sides agree on the question, what already exists, and what a useful first step looks like. This brief puts that in writing without turning every partnership into paperwork.

## Sections

- **The problem** — a specific need, question or defect, with public evidence.
- **What exists already** — prior work, prototypes, licences, open questions.
- **Proposed contribution** — the smallest checkable action and what "done" means.
- **Ownership, credit and permissions** — authorship, licences and consent.
- **Review and next steps** — who reviews, how success is tested, what would stop it.

Use it for a research correction, a design hand-off or a small open-source contribution. Remove personal details before posting it anywhere public. If you'd like to work with me, you can attach it to an [offer to help](/collaborate/#projects).

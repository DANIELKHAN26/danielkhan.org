A change in law is not the same as its enforcement, and a budget allocation is not proof that a service reached people. This programme organises research around those distinctions: each question must be answerable from identifiable evidence, and each piece states what the evidence could **not** establish.

## Method

- A chronology of the relevant decisions.
- A source register with dates, definitions and denominators.
- The institutions responsible for each decision.
- The strongest competing explanation, tested rather than dismissed.

Finished work is published with source notes and a correction history. Working files and anything that could identify participants stay private. The [evidence ledger](/lab/evidence-ledger/) used here is free in the Lab.

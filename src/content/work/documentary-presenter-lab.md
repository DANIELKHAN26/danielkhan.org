A listener cannot reread a sentence. This lab develops a repeatable route from evidence file to finished video — spoken script, teleprompter rehearsal, recording and review — so that a researched argument keeps its shape when it becomes speech.

The spoken script holds only the words to be said; charts, sources and production cues live in a separate production copy. Two tools from this work are free in the Lab: the [script timer](/tools/script-timer/) and the [production card](/lab/production-card/).

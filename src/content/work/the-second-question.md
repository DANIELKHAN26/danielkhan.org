A number can be true without answering the question most people think it answers. A service existing and a service working reliably are different things. The first is easier to count: a connection installed, a school seat recorded, an office opened. The second asks what happens when someone actually needs it.

## A better public conversation

Instead of arguing over whether the headline number is false, ask what was counted. Was the figure a national average, a forecast, or the result of a particular survey? Does the standard require the service to be accessible, affordable and functioning — or only present on a register?

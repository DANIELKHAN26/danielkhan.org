Words that scroll faster than a presenter can think produce a word-perfect reading that sounds disconnected. ScriptFlow shows a short, complete thought close to the camera line and lets the presenter decide when to move on.

## Planned features

- Line-by-line and scrolling modes, controlled by the presenter.
- Mistake markers during recording, to find retakes quickly.
- A review record comparing what was said with the approved script.
- Fully offline — unpublished scripts never leave the computer.

The prototype is being tested; a public repository will follow once it passes release checks.

Much public information now arrives as video: speeches, interviews, broadcast clips and short-form posts. Most verification tools are built for text. This experiment works on the video end of that gap.

## What it explores

- **Transcription** of news video, with timestamps so every claim can be traced to the moment it was said.
- **Claim extraction** — separating checkable statements of fact from opinion and commentary.
- **Source checking** — matching each claim against primary records and published fact-checks.
- **Analysis** — how claims are framed, repeated or changed across clips and outlets.

## Ground rules

Retrieving a matching source is not the same as verifying a claim. The design keeps the evidence visible and leaves the final judgement to a human reviewer. Results will be shared here only once they have been tested.
